RESEARCH NOTES · 16 OCTOBER 2026

Inside the Superfast.

Reference study for the brakes, frame, powertrain and supporting systems in this independent reconstruction of the 812 Superfast coupé.

Published rotor dimensions are reproduced in the mesh. Other component shapes, mounting coordinates and routes are reconstructed from assembly diagrams and the available body envelope. This is a visual study, with a record of its sources and inspection limits.

Evidence used

  • Ferrari launch technical document: engine architecture and vehicle dimensions.
  • Ferrari 2017 annual report: front engine/rear transaxle, electric front steering and rear steering architecture.
  • Superfast technical sheet, archived by SGCarMart: 398 × 38 mm front and 360 × 32 mm rear brakes.
  • 812 Superfast assembly catalogue: component relationships, separate castings and routed systems.

Inspection

The assembled export is checked for triangle intersections between component groups, followed by review of each finding. Fastening interfaces, embedded seals, shaft bearings and component housings require interpretation. Caliper/pad clearance is checked against the rotor envelope; spoke clearance is evaluated over the caliper's radial range.

The review includes the closed exterior, opened doors/hood, cabin and exposed structure. Routes use bounded rounded elbows to avoid spline overshoot. Fixed rear body returns are separated from the moving door skins.

What remains approximate

Factory CAD, measured suspension hardpoints and production tolerances are unavailable. Pistons/crank/cams, internal gearbox teeth/clutches, airbag mechanisms, detailed HVAC ductwork, latch/window mechanisms and individual electrical circuits are not reproduced. The original body remains an artist-authored CC0 asset; cockpit surfaces are based on the supplied photographs. This inspection does not certify fully enclosed volume collisions, a complete motion sweep or engineering performance.
