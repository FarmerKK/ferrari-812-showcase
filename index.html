ABOUT THE ATELIER

Inspired by the 812.
Built for discovery.

This independent experience uses the artist-authored Ferrari 812 Superfast mesh by Jayrenn Reeve, licensed CC0 through BlenderKit. The body, glazing and lamps follow that asset. The cockpit is rebuilt to the supplied interior reference with sculpted leather seats, stitched trim, a detailed steering wheel and illuminated instruments. It is not an official Ferrari product or engineering CAD model.

The car is prepared at the published overall length. The brake mesh uses published rotor dimensions. The engine, transaxle, suspension, frame and routed systems are reconstructed from Superfast assembly references; mounting coordinates and casting shapes remain approximate. Read the structure study and inspection notes. Scanned leather surface maps from ambientCG (CC0), area lighting and contact shading improve close-up material detail. Gauge readouts and cockpit controls are showroom illustrations. The sound study uses edited CC0 Ferrari idle and acceleration recordings by jtvdb and biholao; their recordists do not identify an 812 Superfast. Sound starts on request and stops when the tab is hidden. The continuous roof and rear quarters remain one body shell. Opening angles and the fictional circuit are illustrative.

Import a licensed, self-contained GLB. Parts use semantic mesh names or userData.partId; unknown meshes remain static. Export captures the current assembled configuration. Source artist: Jayrenn Reeve / BlenderKit, CC0.
